# ART-VISION.md — 25 moves toward the House of Bengal

The standard: a maison-grade page — history, culture, nature, restraint. The
buyer in Dubai should feel the owner spent thousands on this design because
every element earns its place. What is DONE below shipped 2026-07-19; the rest
is the roadmap, in priority order within each act.

## Act I — The stage (grounds & light)

1. **One artifact per chapter.** ✅ DONE — tiger owns Origin, fisherman owns
   Markets, rickshaw owns the CTA, nouka owns the portal and the footer,
   shaplas flank the numbers. Never two heroes in one room; keep this law as
   new sections appear.
2. **The cloth must whisper.** ✅ DONE — jamdani/lotus weave halved and
   enlarged, CTA damask cut to .08. Next: let the weave *breathe* — a 90s
   opacity swell of ±15%, like candlelight on fabric.
3. **Cleared ground is the luxury.** ✅ DONE for the tiger (the empty strip it
   walks is bought, deliberate space). Extend the same rule: when real product
   photography arrives, give the banner photo one cleared quadrant with no
   text at all.
4. **Light falls from one direction.** All glows are warm-gold from above-left
   today by accident; codify it — every drop-shadow/halo angle identical, the
   way a jeweller lights one vitrine.
5. **The gild filter is the house varnish.** ✅ DONE (sepia-warm filter turns
   pale engravings gold). Apply it to every future raster so all art shares
   one metal.

## Act II — The engravings (using the client's art fully)

6. **The tiger's eye-line.** The tiger walks INTO the page — content sits
   where it looks. If the About layout ever flips, flip the tiger; the animal
   must always face the story, never the exit.
7. **A tiger appearance is an event.** It appears ONCE. Resist every tempting
   second tiger; scarcity is what makes it heraldic.
8. **The fisherman's net wants a catch.** At Markets, one gold route-line from
   the map could land inside the net's arc — art and data touching once.
   Subtle, one line only.
9. **The rickshaw invites boarding.** At the CTA it stands still; on hover of
   "Request a Bulk Quote", let its wheel turn 15° once. A wink, not a cartoon.
10. **The nouka completes a day.** Morning: sailing in the hero portal.
    Night: leaving in the footer, slightly smaller, lower. Consider a faint
    moon in the footer weave so the pair reads as sunrise/سها moonset.
11. **Missing plates from the sheet art.** The ornament sheets in `bg/` hold
    an uncut golden ARCH, paddy corners, and a sack still-life. Cut the arch
    for the 404/empty states; the paddy corners for the line-card PDF header.

## Act III — Type & word (history in the letterforms)

12. **A Bengali word, once.** One phrase — “বাংলার ফসল, বিশ্বের টেবিলে” — set
    small in a fine Bengali serif (e.g. Tiro Bangla) under the footer brand.
    One line of mother tongue signs the whole house; more would be wallpaper.
13. **Numerals as jewellery.** The chapter numerals (01–05) deserve Marcellus
    with a hairline ring, like coin dies. Same die every chapter.
14. **The pull-quote voice.** All italics-gold lines should sound like one
    narrator: the delta speaking. Audit copy so metaphors stay riverine
    (harvest, current, tide, ghat) — never generic biz-speak.
15. **Letter-spacing discipline.** Micro-caps everywhere run .22–.42em today;
    normalize to two fixed tracks (labels .24em, rails .38em) so the page has
    a typographic pulse, not noise.
16. **The ampersand test.** Cormorant's italic ampersand is gorgeous — allow
    it in headings ("Rice & the River"); never in body copy.

## Act IV — Ritual & motion (nothing moves without meaning)

17. **The alpona draws only once per visit.** Repeat visitors should see it
    already drawn (sessionStorage) — a craftsman doesn't redraw his floor for
    the same guest twice.
18. **Scroll is the river.** The one long-term signature worth building: a
    hairline gold "waterline" in the page margin that flows downward as you
    scroll, passing each chapter's artifact like banks of a river.
19. **Hover = lamplight.** Unify all hovers to one gesture: a warm glow rises
    (never scale-jumps, never color-swaps). The rickshaw wheel (9) is the sole
    licensed exception.
20. **Sound of the house — silence.** No autoplay video, no chimes, ever. In
    a bazaar of noisy exporter sites, stillness IS the luxury tell.

## Act V — Matter & proof (what makes buyers believe the spend)

21. **Real photography, art-directed.** When the client shoots: golden hour,
    low sun, hands in frame (harvest, sorting, ice) — match the engravings'
    warmth so photos and plates hang in one gallery. Provide a one-page shot
    list before the shoot.
22. **The seal becomes physical.** ✅ seal exists in the banner. Next: emboss
    it as the line-card PDF's letterhead mark and as the WhatsApp OG card —
    one seal across paper, screen, and chat.
23. **An Arabic mirror, done properly.** For Dubai: a true RTL Arabic edition
    with a matched Arabic serif — not a toggle bolted on. The arch, the tiger
    and the gold translate; that's the point of building on ornament, not
    on words.
24. **Certificates as framed documents.** When numbers arrive, render each
    cert as a small framed plate (hairline + scooped corners like the product
    cards) — proof displayed the way the house displays everything else.
25. **The 100th detail: a colophon.** A final footer line — "Designed in the
    delta. Set in Cormorant & Marcellus. Woven in jamdani." Maisons sign
    their work; the signature tells buyers every pixel was chosen.
