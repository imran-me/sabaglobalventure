# PHOTO-SHOT-LIST.md — the one-day shoot that finishes the site

(ART-VISION #21.) Give this page to whoever holds the camera — a hired
photographer or a nephew with a recent phone. Follow it exactly and every
photo will hang beside the golden engravings as one gallery. Shoot over ONE
golden hour if possible (the hour after sunrise or before sunset).

## The three rules (non-negotiable)

1. **Golden hour only.** Low warm sun, long shadows. Midday light makes
   commodity photos; evening light makes maison photos. Overcast day = don't
   shoot, wait.
2. **Hands in frame.** Every product shot needs a working hand — holding,
   pouring, sorting, lifting. Hands are the trust signal no stock photo has.
3. **Warm + dark backgrounds.** Wood, jute, clay, water, dusk sky. NEVER
   white tile, plastic crates in frame center, or fluorescent light. The site
   grades everything toward navy-and-gold; shoot warm and it will sit rightly.

Phone settings: highest resolution, NO flash, NO beauty/HDR filters, tap to
focus on the product, hold for 2 seconds. Landscape (wide) orientation for
banners, portrait for product cards. Take every shot 3 times.

## A. The hero portal (1 photo, the most important)

- A nouka or working boat on the river at golden hour, low angle from the
  bank, sun behind or beside the boat. A person aboard (silhouette is fine).
- Portrait orientation, boat in the lower half, sky above.
- This replaces the engraving inside the arched portal on the first screen.

## B. Product photography (one per SKU, portrait, for the cards)

For each: golden hour or warm indoor lamp, product filling ~70% of frame,
a hand in action, background dark/warm and slightly out of focus.

1. **Miniket rice** — a hand plunging into an open jute sack, grains falling.
2. **Chinigura rice** — a brass or clay bowl heaped, a hand sprinkling grains.
3. **Parboiled rice** — grains pouring from a scoop into a sack.
4. **Black tiger shrimp** — on crushed ice in a woven basket, one held up.
5. **Vannamei shrimp** — a full sorting tray, wet, a gloved hand grading.
6. **Golda prawn** — one large prawn held toward the lens, river behind.
7. **Hilsa** — whole fish on ice, scales catching the light; a fisherman's
   hand steadying it.
8. **Rui & katla** — the day's catch in a basket at the landing ghat.
9. **Shutki (dried fish)** — hanging rows against the dusk sky (this one is
   a signature shot — shoot it wide too).
10. **Green chilli** — a heaped basket, one handful held out.
11. **Bitter gourd / korola** — freshly picked, dew or water droplets.
12. **Leafy greens** — bundled, held up by the tie, field behind.
13. **Haribhanga mango** — a hand holding two, orchard light behind.
14. **Jackfruit** — one whole + one opened showing pods, on a wooden table.
15. **Green coconut** — a stack, one being trimmed with a dao (machete).
16. **Turmeric** — whole fingers in one bowl, ground powder in another, a
    hand dusting the powder.
17. **Dried red chilli** — a red field of chillies drying, a hand raking.
18. **Table potato** — sacks open at a warehouse, one potato held to camera.
19. **Lentils & pulses** — three bowls (masoor, mug, khesari), a hand
    letting lentils rain down.
20. **Date-palm gur** — cakes stacked on a clay plate, one broken to show
    the grain.

## C. The story chapter (2–3 photos)

- **Harvest loading at a river ghat** — wide, boats + sacks + workers at
  golden hour. This fills the arched portrait in "Rooted in the Delta".
- **The warehouse** — rows of stacked sacks, one worker walking the aisle,
  shafts of light.
- **Quality control** — hands sieving/grading grain at a table, close up.

## D. The people (2 photos)

- **The Managing Director** — waist-up, warm side light, dark background,
  looking at camera, no forced smile. Kurta or shirt — dignified, not
  corporate. This goes beside the signed note in the story chapter.
- **The team at the ghat/warehouse** — one candid working group shot.

## E. Deliverables checklist

- [ ] 1 hero boat (portrait)
- [ ] 20 product shots (portrait, one per SKU above)
- [ ] 3 story shots (wide/landscape)
- [ ] 2 people shots
- [ ] Everything at full resolution, unedited — the site does the grading.

Upload originals to a drive folder; the site's Admin → Products takes each
photo from there. Do not compress in WhatsApp — WhatsApp destroys them; use
Google Drive or email attachments.
