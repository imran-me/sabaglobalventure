The House

Built for Bulk, Backed by Care

Six things we will not compromise, whatever the container holds.

  1. i

    Direct Sourcing

    We buy at the farm gate and the landing station — no layers of middlemen between the crop and your container.

  2. ii

    Quality Control

    Export-graded, sortex-clean and spec-matched. Inspected and sampled before a single carton is sealed.

  3. iii

    Sea & Air Freight

    FCL and reefer out of Chattogram; air freight out of Dhaka for produce that cannot wait. FOB and CIF terms.

  4. iv

    Unbroken Cold Chain

    Blast-frozen at source, pre-cooled at pack, monitored in transit. Shrimp and produce arrive as they left.

  5. v

    Custom Packaging

    Pack sizes, jute or PP, cartons, mesh or vacuum — and private label built for your shelf, not ours.

  6. vi

    Trade Documentation

    Halal, phytosanitary, health certificate, CoO and the full export file — handled end to end, on time.
